# Clue's tracking options, read on the 6T

Research notes, as of 2026-11-14. These are not a verdict.

**How the list was read:**
- Clue's daily tracking sheet, which the owner had opened on the OnePlus 6T.
- Read from screen dumps while scrolling on the empty right margin. Nothing was tapped or saved, and the sheet was scrolled back to the top.
- The owner's own custom tags were on the sheet. They are left out of these notes on purpose.

**On the screen:** Search tracking options, a Customize button, and Learn more links.

## Observations

- **An explicit "nothing today" option** in many groups (Pain free, OK, None, None today). This tells "fine" apart from "not logged", which matters for the "x of y" counts.
- **Almost nothing has a severity.** Only Hot flashes and Energy are scales. Pain has locations but no intensity, and Feelings is 14 words with no scale.
- **Energy is a 5-step scale,** from Exhausted to Fully energized. It treats the top as good, with no "restless" end.
- **Sex drive sits inside Sex life,** where the owner wants two trackers.
- **Some universal symptoms only appear in the pregnancy groups:** dizziness, heart palpitations, nose bleed, swollen feet, leg cramps.
- **Bundles:** Perimenopause is a preset that switches on options from other groups.
- **Birth control devices** are dated events (inserted, removed, replaced, thread checked), not daily entries.
- **Medicines and supplements are generic one-tap classes,** with no product, dose or time.
- **Custom tags carry information the model lacks:** a clock time typed into the tag name (one tag per hour), and body locations Pain doesn't offer.
